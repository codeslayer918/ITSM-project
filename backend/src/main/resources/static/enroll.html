ITSM mTLS Lab - enrol your certificate

Lost certificate?

1. Generate your key pair locally

The private key is created on your machine and never sent to the server. Only the CSR (public key + signature proving you own the private key) is uploaded. The subject of the CSR is ignored: the CA uses your account.

openssl genpkey -algorithm EC -pkeyopt ec_paramgen_curve:P-256 -out key.pem
openssl req -new -key key.pem -subj /CN=csr -out csr.pem

Or do everything at once: ./scripts/enroll.sh --token <token> --out clients/<you>

2. Submit the CSR with your invitation token
